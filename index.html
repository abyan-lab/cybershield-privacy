CyberShield Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

CyberShield ("the app") is a mobile security helper that checks links and QR codes, and helps you review your own security habits. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we handle

  • Account details: your name, email address and a password you choose, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Scan history: the links you check and the results, so you can look at them again later.
  • App settings: your theme choice and whether the biometric app lock is switched on.

Links you check

When you scan a link, the app first checks it with its own offline rules on your device. It may then send that link to Google Safe Browsing to see whether Google lists it as unsafe. Only the link is sent. Google's handling of that request is described in Google's own privacy policy. Google cannot guarantee that its information is complete or error-free, so some risky sites may not be identified and some safe sites may be flagged in error.

Password leak check

If you use the password leak check, the first 5 characters of a one-way hash of the password are sent to haveibeenpwned.com to see whether it appears in known leaks. The password itself is never sent and never leaves your phone.

Camera

The camera is used only to read QR codes when you choose the QR scan option. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded.

Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap the paste button in the link field.

Biometric lock

If you turn on the app lock, the app asks Android to verify your fingerprint or face. The app never receives or stores your biometric data. Android handles it.

Where your data is kept

Your account details, scan history and settings are stored on your device. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not share your data with third parties, except for the link check with Google Safe Browsing and the optional password leak check described above.

Deleting your data

In Settings you can clear your scan history at any time, and you can delete your account, which removes your account data from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes the data stored on your device.

Children

CyberShield is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
